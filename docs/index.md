# Soup Quiz — Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026.

Soup Quiz is a local-first, offline quiz game. This policy is short because the app's
privacy story is simple.

**What we collect: nothing.** The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no
trackers, and it makes no network requests to us or to any third party. There are no
accounts and no server — it works entirely offline. Tapping an external link (support,
a soup's source, an image credit) opens that website in your browser; from there the
destination's own privacy policy applies.

**Your data stays on your device.** All progress — rounds, answers, streaks, and
daily-challenge results — lives in a local database on your device and never leaves it.
"Clear stats" in the app deletes it permanently; uninstalling the app deletes it too.

**Permissions.** The app asks for notification permission only if you turn on the
optional daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no server is involved.
No other permissions are used.

**Content and credits.** Quiz content is compiled from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) with
per-item source attribution and image credits, visible in the app. This policy covers
the app itself, not those sources.

**Contact.** Questions or concerns: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/stilkin/soup-quiz/issues)
or reach us via [ko-fi.com/stilkin](https://ko-fi.com/stilkin).

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with a new effective date.
